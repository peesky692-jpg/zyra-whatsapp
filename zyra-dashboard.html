FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is ZYRA Business?+
ZYRA Business is an AI-powered business automation platform. It handles customer messages, bookings, admin, and content — so you can focus on growing your business.
Do I need technical skills?+
No. ZYRA Business is designed for business owners, not developers. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use ZYRA Business. Setup takes less than 5 minutes.
Can I start for free?+
Yes! ZYRA's free plan gives you everything to get started — no credit card required. Upgrade only when your business needs more.
How does billing work?+
You're billed monthly and can cancel anytime with no penalty. If you upgrade mid-month, we prorate the charge automatically.
Does it work on my phone?+
Yes. ZYRA works fully on mobile. Manage your entire business from your phone — customers, bookings, AI replies, and analytics all in one place.
What languages does ZYRA support?+
ZYRA responds in 50+ languages including English, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, French and more. It detects the customer's language automatically.
